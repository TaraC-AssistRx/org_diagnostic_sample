✨ AI Flight Risk Methodology

Scores (0–100) are weighted: Survey Sentiment (35%) — 30/60/90-day new hire surveys for recent hires; for tenured employees, proxied from performance trajectory vs. compa ratio; Compensation Pressure (25%) — compa ratio below 0.95 with high performance dramatically increases risk; Tenure Pattern (20%) — peak attrition windows at 2–4 yrs and 8–10 yrs; Role Fit (20%) — time in level, span awareness, dotted-line complexity. Scores ≥65 = High, 40–64 = Medium, <40 = Low.
